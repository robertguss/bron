# ADR 0013 — Partners: outreach after the thin slice exists

**Status:** Accepted — 2026-11-02

## Context

Bron builds over libraries others assembled (the NRI's Kuyper and Bavinck archives, PRDL, Delpher, DBNL) and needs scholars' trust to matter. The question was when to make first contact.

## Decision

- **No outreach until the thin slice exists:** one chapter of Bavinck's *Dogmatiek*, both editions, aligned, with English alongside, provenance visible, on a public URL. The first email contains a thing, not a plan.
- **Order after that:** Dmytro Bintsarovskyi at the NRI first (the second-edition pages and the bibliography are his; Bron links back to them), then the Bavinck Institute at Calvin Seminary, then Westminster faculty for the reviewer list, then CCEL as precedent and ally, then the Junius Institute when the scholastics phase begins.
- **Posture:** Bron is the open engine over their libraries, links back to them, and asks for nothing until it has shown something. Specific asks come with evidence (e.g. "here is our structural concordance; does your second-edition text exist in a form we could align against?").
- **Until then:** build on public sources only (Delpher, DBNL, Gutenberg, the NRI's public pages), credit them in every file, and keep notes of what to ask whom.

## Consequences
- The thin slice becomes the first external milestone, not only the first internal one.
- Nothing in the design depends on a partner saying yes.
